Books on Aqua

How one wallet backs every book

The maker approves Aqua once and ships a Long and a Short book to every market. Each book gets a virtual allocation: nothing leaves the wallet when a book is shipped, so the allocations together can exceed the approval.

When a taker trades, Aqua moves only what that fill needs — for a buy, the taker's payment in and the tUSDC to mint the new Long + Short pair out. The other books are not touched. Risk across all markets is capped by one budget (capital utilization U).
